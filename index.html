abragmania

Dashboards for sports and markets, built for daily use.

NFL Depth Charts

/nfl-depth-charts-site

Offense and defense for all 32 teams: a depth chart per team with logos, headshots, jersey numbers, ratings and injury status, plus an analytics view of usage, production and red-zone tiers by position.

Mowgli

/mowgli-site

An equity-analysis dashboard: a live market monitor with sector heat maps and breadth, and a page per company with how it makes money, valuation, financials, regions and what is next.

NFL DFS Rankings

/nfl-dfs-rankings-site

Weekly DraftKings rankings for classic slates and showdowns: projections, ownership, value and leverage signals, and a lineup builder.

The Chop

/thechop-site

Every MLB player at a glance: how good he is, what he does and how he does it, across every season of his career. Data from FanGraphs.

Pickle RR

/Pickle_RPRR

A free 8-player rotating-partner pickleball round robin: enter names, score 14 games over 7 rounds, then playoffs, finals and payouts. Installs to a phone and works offline.
